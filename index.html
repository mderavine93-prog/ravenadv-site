The details you send us by email, phone or the contact form on this page (the form opens your own email app; nothing is stored on this site); and business documents, such as bank statements, that you choose to send us by email after speaking with an advisor. Please do not send bank statements, Social Security numbers, EINs or account numbers through the website form.

How we use it. To respond to you, review your business’s funding options, match your request with funding partners, keep records of our conversations and send follow-ups about your request.

Who we share it with. With funding partners only as needed to review your request, with service providers that help us run our business (such as email and scheduling tools), and when required by law. We do not sell or rent your personal information.
